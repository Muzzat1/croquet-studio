Seasonal Lawn Dynamics

Winter Considerations (May – August) Low morning sun angles and heavy overnight dew mean lawns dry slowly. At 7:00–8:00 am, cold turf dampens ball rebound and drastically slows down lag shots. Lower apparent temperatures can cause cold, stiff hands, impacting mallet grip and swing touch.
Shoulder Seasons (Apr / Sep – Oct) Generally ideal playing conditions. Morning dew burns off briskly between 8:00 am and 9:00 am, leaving a fast, consistent surface without excessive heat stress.
Summer Considerations (Nov – March) Lawns transition rapidly from heavy morning dew to dry, baked surfaces. By 10:00 am, rigid hoops reject off-centre shots aggressively. High humidity restricts sweat evaporation, requiring frequent shade and hydration.
